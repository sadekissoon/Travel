Foundations HTML + CSS Project

The Digital Explorer's Travel Map

Pack your digital bags and hover across the map to discover five dream destinations, each with a photo, a fun fact, and a little CSS magic.

1

Kyoto, Japan

Lanterns & Temple Trails

Did you know Kyoto has more than 1,600 Buddhist temples and over 400 Shinto shrines?

2

Santorini, Greece

Cliffs Above the Aegean

Santorini's famous crescent shape was formed by one of history's largest volcanic eruptions.

3

Banff, Canada

Glacier Blue Adventure

The lake water in Banff looks bright blue because rock flour from glaciers reflects sunlight.

4

Marrakech, Morocco

Markets of Color

Marrakech is nicknamed the Red City because many of its buildings glow with warm red clay walls.

5

Bora Bora, French Polynesia

Lagoon Daydream

Bora Bora is surrounded by a coral reef that creates its calm, glowing turquoise lagoon.

Built with HTML5, CSS, hover effects, images, and a sense of adventure.
